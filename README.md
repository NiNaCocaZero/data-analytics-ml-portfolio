##  Core Portfolio Projects

### 1. Financial Credit Risk Analytics & Multi-Model Benchmarking
* **Domain:** Corporate Finance & Risk Management (Taiwan Credit Dataset - 30,000 records)
* **Framework:** Supervised Binary Classification (Logistic Regression vs. Decision Trees vs. Random Forest)
* **Technical Highlights:** Mitigated extreme class imbalance using automated cost-sensitive learning (`class_weight='balanced'`). Engineered domain-specific metrics (`UTILIZATION_RATE`) to isolate financial exposure thresholds.
* **Business Outcome:** Selected **Random Forest** as the champion deployment model, stabilizing the precision-recall frontier to capture high-risk defaulters (**0.761 ROC-AUC**) while drastically reducing operational false alarms for the bank's risk assessment team.
* **Keywords:** `Python`, `Scikit-Learn`, `Class Imbalance`, `Feature Engineering`, `ROC-AUC`, `Financial Risk`

### 2. Customer Segmentation via RFM Behavioral Clustering
* **Domain:** E-Commerce & Growth Marketing Operations
* **Framework:** Unsupervised Machine Learning (K-Means Clustering)
* **Technical Highlights:** Implemented Feature Standardization via `StandardScaler` to remove distance metric bias. Applied dual-metric optimization incorporating the **Elbow Method (Inertia)** and **Silhouette Coefficient Analysis** to establish deterministic cluster boundaries at K=3.
* **Business Outcome:** Segmented the consumer population into three highly-actionable operational tiers: *Dormant/At-Risk Customers* (targeted for automated win-back coupon triggers), *High-Value Loyalists* (allocated to premium loyalty workflows), and *Promising Recent Cores* (targeted for cross-selling algorithms).
* **Keywords:** `K-Means`, `Unsupervised Learning`, `RFM Model`, `Silhouette Score`, `Customer Lifecycle`

### 3. Healthcare Churn Predictive Modeling & Patient Retention
* **Domain:** Private Healthcare Insurance & Customer Success
* **Framework:** Ensemble Machine Learning (Random Forest Classifier)
* **Technical Highlights:** Stratified partitions to manage multi-variable demographic and utilization telemetry. Evaluated predictive frontiers via continuous **Precision-Recall Curves** to isolate high-risk attrition clusters. Extracting structural **Feature Importance** matrices to open model architecture.
* **Business Outcome:** Proved that patient attrition was highly sensitive to customer service friction vectors (`Customer_Service_Calls`) rather than pricing parameters, enabling customer success groups to trigger real-time mitigation playbooks before full subscription cycles terminated.
* **Keywords:** `Random Forest`, `Ensemble Learning`, `Precision-Recall Curve`, `Feature Importance`, `Churn Mitigation`

### 4. Predictive Sales Forecasting & Supply Chain Optimization
* **Domain:** Retail Operations & Revenue Forecasting
* **Framework:** Multivariate Linear Regression (Ordinary Least Squares - OLS)
* **Technical Highlights:** Conducted comprehensive multivariate Pearson correlation diagnostics to isolate price elasticity and seasonal trends without introducing multi-collinearity variance.
* **Business Outcome:** Achieved an **$R^2$ coefficient of 0.960** with a **Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 98.76 units**, providing logistics and warehouse managers with an explicit variance buffer for automated stock reordering workflows, drastically minimizing dead inventory overheads.
* **Keywords:** `Linear Regression`, `Pearson Correlation`, `Demand Forecasting`, `MAE`, `Supply Chain Optimization`

### 6. SpaceX Falcon 9 Landing Prediction & Multi-Model Analytics
* **Domain:** Aerospace Engineering & Advanced Predictive Analytics (IBM Capstone)
* **Framework:** Supervised Classification (Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, SVM, KNN) & Geospatial Intelligence
* **Technical Highlights:** Extracted and cleaned multi-source telemetry data using `BeautifulSoup` (web scraping) and SpaceX `REST APIs`. Built interactive geospatial maps (`Folium`) to evaluate launch site proximity factors and developed dynamic `Plotly/Dash` dashboards. Optimized model hyper-parameters via `GridSearchCV`.
* **Business Outcome:** Developed a robust predictive architecture to determine first-stage rocket landing success, directly enabling cost-efficiency analysis for commercial rocket launch bidding strategies against legacy aerospace competitors.
* **Keywords:** `Python`, `Web Scraping`, `Rest API`, `Folium`, `GridSearchCV`, `Classification Algorithms`, `Predictive Analytics`
