## Dashboard Analysis

The Power BI dashboard provides an executive view of sales performance, operational volume, customer behavior, geographic distribution, and temporal trends.

### 1. Financial & Sales Metrics

* **Total Revenue:** Gross sales revenue calculated from `Quantity × Unit Price`.
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** Average revenue generated per unique invoice, calculated as total revenue divided by the number of unique invoices.
* **Average Unit Price:** Average selling price across the products included in the cleaned dataset.

### 2. Operational & Volume Metrics

* **Total Quantity:** Total number of product units sold.
* **Total Orders / Transactions:** Number of unique invoices, providing a measure of transaction volume.
* **Total SKUs / Unique Products:** Number of distinct products represented in the dataset.
* **Transaction Volume:** Analysis of the scale and distribution of sales activity across the available transactions.

### 3. Customer & Geographic Analysis

* **Unique Customers:** Number of distinct customers represented in the dataset.
* **Revenue by Country:** Geographic distribution of revenue, highlighting the contribution of the UK market relative to international markets.
* **Top Customers:** Identification of customers generating the highest revenue contribution.
* **Top Products:** Identification of the highest-performing products based on both units sold and revenue generated.

### 4. Temporal Analysis

* **Revenue by Month / Year:** Analysis of revenue evolution over time to identify trends, peaks, and potential seasonal patterns.
* **Purchase Patterns:** Analysis of transaction activity across different days and time periods to identify concentration patterns in customer purchasing behavior.
